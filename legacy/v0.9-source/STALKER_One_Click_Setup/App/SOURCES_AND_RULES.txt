IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This build enforces magazine FAMILY matching for its Tier 1 catalogue. Legacy custom items with
  no magazine-family metadata still fall back to earlier caliber-based matching for compatibility.
- Fixed/integral feeds are represented as an attached non-ejectable magazine item; compatible ammunition
  can be loaded via the Ammo & Magazines tab.
- Magazine capacities and image cell dimensions follow the supplied game screenshot, not necessarily
  current real-world or original video-game statistics.
- Weights and GURPS combat values are approximate player-facing starting points, not verified official stats.
- Image thumbnails are crops of the uploaded collage, not isolated transparent renders.
